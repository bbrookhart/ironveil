# Research integrity

- VEILNODE, device classes, dependencies, vulnerabilities, mission functions, and industrial data are fictional.
- Firmware is synthetic and is never flashed to hardware.
- Adversarial scenarios mutate only deterministic local fixtures.
- No vendor infrastructure, external registry, identity provider, or production build system is attacked or impersonated.
- SLSA levels are not self-awarded; unverified properties remain `NOT_VERIFIED`.
- Simulated target measurement is not hardware root-of-trust verification.
- SBOM findings and metrics depend on the included tooling and explicit ground truth.
- Provenance and signing do not prove that software is benign or vulnerability-free.
- TUF/Uptane design references are not conformance claims.
- No government or standards-body endorsement is implied.
