# NIST C-SCRM mapping

Baseline: NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1 Update 1 (final).

IRONVEIL implements synthetic supplier/component identity, digest-bound provenance, composition
transparency, acquisition-style evidence gates, vulnerability/VEX records, lifecycle inventory,
and component-to-mission impact analysis. It does not evaluate real suppliers or establish NIST
conformance.
