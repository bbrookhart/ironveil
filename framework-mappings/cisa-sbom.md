# CISA SBOM Minimum Elements mapping

Baseline: CISA 2026 Minimum Elements, which replaced the 2025 guidance on July 29, 2026.

The synthetic CycloneDX evidence records the SBOM author/tool, producer context, component names and
versions, package identifiers, hashes, licenses, dependency relationships, timestamp, and generation
context. The verifier additionally compares the declaration to an explicit ground-truth fixture and
reports precision and recall.
